Two trips in, and the thing I would tell anyone going is that the day trips beat the cities. The
train network is excellent and it makes the whole country feel close.

## Busan

- **Jagalchi fish market** is fun and worth an hour.
- The most interesting experiences are the **day trips around the city**. The rail network is excellent, and it puts great hikes and scenic spots within easy reach.
- **Gyeongju** is the highlight among them.

## Seoul

- Several **second-hand markets** are worth a morning.
- Two shops I go back to: **Beauty of Joseon** for Korean skincare, and **BlueBlack Penshop** for stationery.

## Still on my list

A museum, any museum. I have managed to visit exactly zero across two trips, which I think says
something about how good the day trips are.
